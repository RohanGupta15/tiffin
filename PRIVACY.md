# Tiffin privacy policy

_Last updated: 14 October 2026_

Tiffin is a new tab and home page for Firefox. It has no account, no analytics and no tracking, and its developer receives none of your data.

## What stays in your browser

- **Your links, lists and settings** (including the name you enter) are saved with Firefox's extension storage. If you use Firefox Sync, Firefox syncs them to your other devices through your Firefox account. Tiffin's developer cannot see them.
- **Search**: when you search from Tiffin, the text goes to the search engine you chose in Firefox, exactly as if you had used the address bar.

## What leaves your browser, and only if you turn on weather

Weather is off until you choose a city in Tiffin's settings. Before the first city search, Firefox asks whether Tiffin may share location information; nothing is sent unless you agree.

With your agreement, Tiffin sends to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com):

- the city name you type, to find matching cities (geocoding), and
- the chosen city's latitude and longitude, rounded to about 100 metres, to get the forecast.

Tiffin never uses your device's location (GPS or Wi-Fi). Open-Meteo is a free weather service that needs no account or key; see its [terms and privacy policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms). Forecasts are cached in your browser for 30 minutes.

To stop sharing, remove the city in Tiffin's settings, or turn off **Location** for Tiffin in `about:addons` → Tiffin → Permissions and data.
